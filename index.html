Gauteng Dog Rescue

Every dog deserves a second chance✦♥

Paws R Us (SA) rescues, shelters, and re-homes abandoned and surrendered dogs across Gauteng. Since 2012, a small dedicated team and a wide network of foster homes have given vulnerable dogs the chance at a loving home.
